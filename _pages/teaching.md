I develop statistics courses and instructional materials, with a focus on making modern Bayesian methods teachable and reproducible.

## Course development

### Bayesian Statistics Module (UCLA, in progress)

I own end-to-end design of a Bayesian statistics module for an upper-division UCLA course. I translate current research literature into lecture material, worked estimation examples, and graded assignments packaged for instructor reuse. Each method ships with an original exposition of its estimation approach and the assumptions under which it holds, plus diagrams of estimator and posterior behavior, sufficient to teach the method from the write-up alone.

Assignments are per-student parameterized — variant parameters hashed from the student identifier, with answers checked against known analytic results — resistant to answer sharing and language-model submissions. The supporting toolchain (see [figure-gate]({{ site.baseurl }}/projects/figure-gate/)) produces the diagrams, and a test-backed Jupyter notebook assignment is deployed in Canvas.

## Prior teaching
