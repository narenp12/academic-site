I'm a statistician and machine learning engineer focused on making statistical inference accessible and reproducible. I recently completed my B.S. in Statistics and Data Science at UCLA, where I worked on Bayesian and sequential inference, anomaly detection, and NLP over scientific corpora.

My research interests lie in **time series forecasting and anomaly detection; Bayesian and sequential inference; topic modeling and NLP over scientific corpora; and reproducible, accessible statistical computing**. Much of my work turns these methods into tools other people can actually use — see my [projects]({{ site.baseurl }}/projects/).

Beyond research, I care about teaching. I've developed a graduate-level Bayesian statistics module for UCLA's Statistics department, built the toolchain that produces its figures and autogrades its assignments, and taught hands-on lab modules for high school students at UCLA's CityLab. More on [teaching]({{ site.baseurl }}/teaching/).
