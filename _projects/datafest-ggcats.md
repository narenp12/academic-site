A DataFest competition project analyzing commercial real estate activity in Chicago, treating the city as two distinct markets rather than one: a burgeoning metropolitan market with strong post-COVID recovery and surging leasing activity, and a cost-effective suburban market with low rental pricing and high-quality buildings.

Team ggcats: Connor Coyne, Madison Ell, Lisa Nguyen, Naren Prakash, Caleb Williams.

## Recommendation

Two regional targets, both with clusters of grade A buildings and stable tenancy:

- **North Michigan Ave metro** — high recent growth
- **Northwest Corridor suburb** — affordable rent, close proximity to the CBD, tenants unlikely to incur additional moving expenses
