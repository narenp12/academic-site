Beta-endorphins are produced by the nervous system to manage pain or stress, and most forms of exercise raise blood beta-endorphin levels after 30 minutes. This project asks which exercise raises them most, and whether the effect holds across age groups.

Design of experiments project with Nathan and Tinn.

## Design

Randomized basic factorial design with two factors and their interaction:

- **Exercise:** freestyle swim, outdoor run, strength training
- **Age group:** 7–25, 26–50, 51+
- **Response:** difference in endorphin level (pg/mL)
- **Held constant:** sex (male), city (Arcadia), duration (30 min)

Degrees of freedom: benchmark 1, exercise 2, age 2, interaction 4, error 189.
